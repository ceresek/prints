# About

Give your pool a spin :-)

Replaces the original return inlet  to give the water in your pool a gentle spin.
Probably also does something when pointed upwards, but I did not try that.

The dimensions should be compatible with the common fittings of the Intex or Marimex pools.

# Printing

Printed with PETG and three perimeters, the spout part with supports.
